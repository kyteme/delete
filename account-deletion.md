# Kyte – Account & Data Deletion Request

Users may request deletion of their Kyte account and associated personal data at any time.

## What will be deleted
- User account and profile information
- Projects, chats, reminders, and events
- Metadata linked to the account

Some data may be retained if required by law.

## How to request deletion
Send an email from your registered email address to:

**legal@kyte.me**

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include your registered email and confirmation of deletion request.

## Processing time
Requests are processed within 30 days.
