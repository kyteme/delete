# Kyte – Account & Data Deletion Request

Kyte respects your right to control your personal data. You may request deletion of your Kyte account and all associated personal data at any time.

## Who can request deletion
Any registered Kyte user.

## What data will be deleted
Upon completion of the request, Kyte will permanently delete:
- User account and profile information (including email address and user ID)
- Projects, chats, reminders, events, and related content
- Metadata associated with the user account

Some data may be retained only if required by applicable law or for legitimate security or compliance purposes, and only for the minimum period required.

## How to request deletion
Send an email from the email address associated with your Kyte account to:

**legal@kyte.me**

**Email subject: Accout Deletion**

**Email body must include:**
- Your registered email address
- A clear statement requesting permanent deletion of your Kyte account and associated personal data

## Processing time
Deletion requests are processed within **30 calendar days** from the date the request is received.

You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is completed.

## Important information
- Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
- After deletion, you will lose access to all Kyte data and services
- Creating a new account in the future will not restore deleted data

## Contact
For questions related to personal data protection or account deletion, please contact:

**legal@kyte.me**
